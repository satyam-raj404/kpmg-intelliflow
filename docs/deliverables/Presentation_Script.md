# IntelliSource — Presenter Script
**Duration:** 3–4 minutes  
**Pace:** Speak steadily. Pause after bold lines. Each [SLIDE] marker = click to advance.

---

## [SLIDE 1 — TITLE]
*(Stand, face the audience, pause 2 seconds before starting.)*

Good morning — what I'm going to show you today is something we built specifically for procurement teams who are tired of pulling five different reports to answer one question.

This is **IntelliSource** — a procurement intelligence platform that gives your finance, procurement, and leadership teams a single live view of everything happening across your purchase-to-pay cycle.

It is currently in active development, and what you'll see today is a working pilot built on real transaction data.

---

## [SLIDE 2 — PROCUREMENT DASHBOARD]

Starting with **Procurement.**

This dashboard is designed for your procurement managers. The moment data is uploaded, every purchase order in the system is classified, tracked, and evaluated.

You can see at a glance which orders are active, which have been raised without an approved requisition — what we call maverick spend — and which orders carry enough value to require an additional layer of approval.

The goal here is simple: your procurement team should not need to run a report to know the state of their orders. It should just be visible.

---

## [SLIDE 3 — FINANCIAL DASHBOARD]  [SLIDE 4 — FINANCIAL TRENDS]

Moving into **Finance.**

This view is built for your finance controller. It shows whether every invoice in the system has a matching purchase order and goods receipt — what procurement calls a three-way match. When that match breaks down, the system surfaces it immediately, before payment is released.

The trend view shows payment volume over time and breaks down whether your payments are going out early, on schedule, or late. This becomes particularly useful during audits and cash flow reviews.

---

## [SLIDE 5 — LEADERSHIP DASHBOARD]  [SLIDE 6 — RISK ANALYTICS]

Now this is the view your leadership team and board would use.

At the top you can see the full procurement pipeline — from purchase requests all the way through to payment — live, in one screen. Below that are the strategic metrics: total committed spend across all departments, your end-to-end cycle time, and the maverick spend rate.

The section I want to draw your attention to is this — **Segregation of Duty conflicts.**

This is something standard reporting does not catch. IntelliSource automatically cross-references who created each document against who approved it, received goods against it, posted the invoice, and cleared the payment. If the same person sits across two of those steps — that is flagged as a control violation. On our pilot data, we found a significant number of these conflicts — and none of them were visible in the underlying system before.

This is the kind of finding that internal audit teams look for. We surface it automatically, with the document number, the vendor, the user, and the date — ready to export.

---

## [SLIDE 7 — VENDOR PERFORMANCE]  [SLIDE 8 — VENDOR CHARTS]

**Vendor Performance** gives your vendor management team a live view of compliance.

You can see which vendors are active, which have a purchasing or payment block, and how each vendor performs on delivery. The spend concentration view shows who your top vendors are — which is useful both for negotiation and for identifying single-source risk.

---

## [SLIDE 9 — CAPEX / OPEX DASHBOARD]

**Utilization** answers the question your budget owners ask every quarter: how much of our spend is capital, and how much is operational?

IntelliSource classifies every purchase order automatically based on the data. No manual spreadsheet. No month-end reconciliation exercise. Just a live split — by category, by department, by month.

---

## [SLIDE 10 — P2P LIFECYCLE TRACKER]

This is the **P2P Lifecycle Tracker** — and this is one of the views that our pilot users found most immediately useful.

It shows every stage of the procurement cycle — from the purchase request, through order approval, goods receipt, invoicing, and final payment — with a health indicator at each stage. Where a stage is green, documents are moving on schedule. Where it turns yellow or red, that is a bottleneck worth investigating.

This makes it very easy to walk into a team meeting and say: here is exactly where our process is slow, and here is how many cases are stuck there.

---

## [SLIDE 11 — DATA UPLOAD]

One practical note: **IntelliSource does not require a system integration** to get started.

You export standard data files from your existing systems — the same files your team is probably already pulling for manual reports — and upload them here. The platform detects the dataset type automatically, validates the data, and refreshes every dashboard within seconds.

This is what made the pilot deployment fast. No IT project. No connectors. No months of integration work.

---
